How to Play
  • The numbers on the left of each row describe how the row's cells should be filled, from left to right.
  • Similarly, the numbers on the top of each column describe how the column's cells should be filled, from top to bottom.
  • Spaces between numbers indicate the presence of at least one empty cell between consecutive filled cells.
  • Example:
    3 2
  • Use the ■ / ✕ toggle at the top to switch between Fill mode and Mark mode.
  • In Fill mode (■): click a cell to fill it; click it again to clear it.
  • In Mark mode (✕): click a cell to mark it with an X as a reminder it should stay empty; click it again to clear the mark.
  • The puzzle is solved when all filled cells match the hidden picture exactly.
