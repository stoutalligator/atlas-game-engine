Nonogram

How to Play
  • The numbers on the left of each row describe consecutive runs of filled cells in that row, in order from left to right, separated by at least one empty cell.
  • The numbers on top of each column describe consecutive runs of filled cells in that column, in order from top to bottom, separated by at least one empty cell.
  • Single-click an empty cell to mark it with an X — a hint that the cell should stay empty.
  • Double-click an empty or X-marked cell to fill it with color.
  • Single-click a filled cell to clear it back to empty.
  • The puzzle is solved when all filled cells match the hidden picture exactly. X marks and empty cells are both treated as "not filled" when checking for a win.
